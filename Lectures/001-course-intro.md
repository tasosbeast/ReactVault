## TL;DR
- This course is designed to serve beginners and advanced learners alike—pace yourself without judging the course too early.
- Actively control your learning experience: replay or skip sections, adjust playback speed, and ask questions as needed.
- Prioritize hands-on coding, progressive understanding, and self-reliant problem solving to build durable React skills.
- Stay patient, review thoroughly between sections, and keep the learning experience enjoyable.

## Eight Habits for Taking the Course
1. **Embrace mixed experience levels.** Expect a broad audience; be patient if lessons feel too fast or slow.
2. **Tune the delivery to your needs.** Rewatch, jump ahead, tweak playback speed, or leverage the Q&A when stuck.
3. **Delay judgment.** Hold off on reviews until you've sampled multiple sections to appreciate the course's full scope.
4. **Curate your path.** You can skip practice-heavy segments when time-constrained, though repetition accelerates mastery.
5. **Code along relentlessly.** [[📚 Concepts/State|Hands-on practice]] is the only path to learning—mirror the instructor's code or experiment.
6. **Focus on making things work first.** In early modules, aim for functioning components before obsessing over best practices.
7. **Debug independently.** Triage issues yourself, search the Q&A archive, then post concise questions with [[Resources/CodeSandbox|CodeSandbox]] links when necessary.
8. **Consolidate before advancing.** Review code, notes, and projects; build small variations to confirm understanding before moving on.
