## Why it matters
Sharing reproductions through [CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io/) gives helpers a running example of your issue without setup friction.

## Usage Tips
- Prefer the "React" starter template so the environment matches course expectations.
- Keep sandbox names descriptive (e.g., `useState-counter-bug`).
- After reproducing the bug, copy the share URL and include it in Q&A posts alongside a short issue summary.
- Update the sandbox when you receive suggestions so helpers can see the latest state.
